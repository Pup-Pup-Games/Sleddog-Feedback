# Code of conduct

This repository is a public space run by a very small studio. The rules are
short, because the standard is not complicated.

## The standard

**Be decent. Assume the person on the other end is trying.**

Criticism of the software is always welcome and often deserved — say the feature
is confusing, say the release broke your session, say the roadmap has the wrong
thing on it. Criticism of *people* is a different thing, and it isn't welcome
here.

Concretely, the things that get a post removed:

- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissiveness aimed at a person rather than at
  the work.
- Harassment of any kind, including sustained argument with someone who has asked
  you to stop.
- Bigotry — anything demeaning someone for who they are rather than what they
  said.
- Sexual content, and content aimed at minors.
- Publishing someone else's private information, including screenshots of a
  table's notes that the table didn't agree to make public.
- Spam, promotion unrelated to Sled Dog, and posts written to farm attention
  rather than to say something.

## What happens

There is one moderator. He is also the developer, and he is not reading this
tracker at 2am.

- A first problem usually gets an edit, a hidden comment, or a reply asking you
  to change how you're saying it.
- Something serious, or a repeat, gets the account blocked from the repository.
  There is no formal appeals process; you can ask for the decision to be
  reconsidered through the contact form.

Moderation decisions here are not a verdict on you as a person. They're a
judgement about one thread on one small repository, made quickly, by someone who
would rather be fixing the app.

## Reporting something

Use the [contact form](https://sleddog.tools/contact) — not a public issue or
reply. Public reports turn a moderation problem into an argument with an
audience, which is worse for everyone, including you.

Tell us what happened and link the thread. You'll get a human answer.

## Scope

This applies to everything in this repository — issues, discussions, comments,
and reactions — and to the other Pup Pup Games repositories that reference it.
It doesn't extend to anyone's conduct elsewhere on the internet, with one
exception: if someone is using another venue to harass a person over something
that happened here, we'll treat that as happening here.
